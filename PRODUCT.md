# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Teachers and students using the LetsStart workspace to ask mathematical questions and understand the reasoning behind the answer.

## Product Purpose
Ganitam Nirmoktra is a personalized mathematical tool. The LetsStart assistant should turn a question into a clear, useful explanation through the project's custom AI model and prompt pipeline.

## Brand Personality
Calm, scholarly, and precise. The assistant should feel encouraging without being chatty, and should make the reasoning easy to follow.

## Anti-references
Avoid noisy dashboard decoration, generic chatbot marketing language, dark neon gradients, and interfaces that hide the primary question-and-answer workflow.

## Design Principles
- Put the mathematical question first.
- Make reasoning readable before adding controls.
- Keep navigation stable while the conversation grows.
- Use the existing visual language and accent colors as functional signals.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Use keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, clear loading and error states, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
